02

Passing

Work down the list as the group settles in
i

Simple

group size dependent
  • Start stationary, then progress to pass + move.
  • Two touches to begin — one to control, one to pass.
  • Proceed to one touch once it's clean.
  • Number of players per group depends on how many turn up.
ii

One marker / cone

both feet
  • Trap with one foot, pass with the other.
  • Ensure the ball stays on the correct side of the marker.
  • Swap the trapping foot so both feet get work.
iii

Two markers / cones

accuracy
  • Now for accuracy — the ball must be passed within the markers to the other player.
  • One or two touches.
  • Narrow the gate as the pair get sharper.
iv

Advanced

optional
  • Pass the ball, then move to the centre.
  • Wait for a pass from another player.
  • Lay off the pass.
  • Move to the other side and repeat.
v

Rondo

keep-away
  • Use two touches — or only one touch to make it harder.
  • Outside players retain the ball.
  • Inside players try to get the ball.
  • Whoever loses it goes in the middle.
03

Dribbling

Comfort first, then speed
i

Simple

out & back
  • Take as many touches as you want during the first half — get comfortable with the ball.
  • Run with the ball on the way back.
  • Fewer touches. More speed.
ii

In + out

one foot, then the other
  • Using one foot, dribble in and out of the markers.
  • Switch feet on the way back.
  • Small touches — keep the ball close to the cones.
iii

In + out — circle

same concept
  • Same concept as above, but in a circle.
  • Round the outside of one cone, the inside of the next.
  • Change direction once you get back to the start.
iv

Advanced

figure-eight
  • Loop out to each outer marker and back through the middle one.
  • Keep the pattern going without stopping the ball.

Variation 1

Inside foot around the middle cone / marker. Outside foot around the outer cones / markers.
